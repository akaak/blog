**Summer 2026:** Nvidia, OpenAI, Oracle, CoreWeave, and AMD are stitching together hundreds of billions in overlapping deals. Analysts call it circular financing. Nvidia pushes back, in public, on the record. The market mostly shrugs it off as aggressive but defensible.

Now, mid-November 2026: Nvidia's CEO has resigned in a fast-moving, 96-hour span, with an investigation still ongoing and no successor named. Same deals, same backstops through 2032 — but the person who built and defended all of it is gone. Here's the circular-deal picture, and why that resignation matters more than it looks.

CoreWeave rents out data-center capacity. Nvidia owns about 5% of CoreWeave. Nvidia sells chips to CoreWeave. And Nvidia has committed to buy any unsold CoreWeave cloud capacity through 2032 — effectively backstopping its own customer.

Supplier, shareholder, and buyer of last resort, all in one relationship. That is the whole circular-deal debate in a single company.

> Imagine a company that sells you equipment, invests money in your business, and then rents back what you build with it. It’s your supplier, your investor and your customer all at once.

- from [Who's paying whom?...](https://theconversation.com/whos-paying-whom-the-circular-money-machine-behind-the-ai-boom-or-bubble-293938)

These kinds of arrangements were made with OpenAI, and Oracle as well. I read that the Morgan Stanley mapped six of these companies and the arrows looked criss-crossed all over.

This is not new. In the dot-com era it was called vendor financing: Lucent lent billions to telecom upstarts so they could buy Lucent's gear. When the customers went bust, Lucent wrote off the debts. As of now, OpenAI is still a private company and if it goes bust then it would hit Nvidia.

None of this proves a bubble. The build-out may pay off if real cash flows eventually justify the spending. The question is not whether AI is real. It is how much of the boom is end-user demand and how much is capital going around in circles — because circularity, as the [WSJ put it on Oct 22, 2025](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/is-the-flurry-of-circular-ai-deals-a-win-winor-sign-of-a-bubble-8a2d70c5), _is virtuous on the upside and vicious on the downside_. The deals work fine, until they don't. That call looks prescient now, over a year before a Nvidia CEO resignation gave the downside case its first real test.

In August 2026, as the circularity questions were first building, Nvidia CFO (and now interim CEO) Colette Kress addressed it head-on: 

> "We recognize the scale of this support, and we know some will call this circular financing. We see it differently,"
...
"The equity returns on our invested capital will be excellent." 

At that time, Kress defended Huang's strategy and publicly stood behind it.

**November 2026:**

_**Then the CEO resigned...**_

...and the picture changed fast. Nvidia had no formal succession plan and no bench of public-facing executives — see [the micro post]({{ site.baseurl }}/micro/2026/11/13/nvidia-ai-super-highway-bump.html) on the 96-hour span itself. Kress, the CFO, is now the one left holding the circular-financing book and accountable for it.

The confidence behind these deals rested on Mr. Huang's judgment and credibility. Now, a few things to watch:

- **Contracts survive** The CoreWeave backstop, the OpenAI stake, the AMD warrants don't disappear with Huang. But, the confidence in seeing through these contracts is a different question.
- **Leadership risk and circularity risk are now the same risk.** Lenders and insurers were already underwriting the chip-backed guarantees. Now they're underwriting Nvidia's leadership too.
- **Everyone in the loop priced in Huang.** OpenAI, CoreWeave, Oracle, and AMD built multi-year bets on an Nvidia led by the one person defending the circularity. Huang successor may redefine this loop.


The circularity question has become even more relevant as that bet was made by Huang and is no longer there. It is to be seen how the new Nvidia management is going to stand behind that bet. Keep, shrink, or unwind?

**THE LOOP:**


`Chip Company (NVIDIA/AMD) -- sells chips/invests --> CoreWeave/OpenAI -- buys services/backstop --> Chip Company (NVIDIA)`
