_**AI Agents & Evals**_

Capability evals are about what you're shooting for: "What can this agent do well?" You start low and hill-climb. Regression evals are about the baseline you've already reached: "Does it still do what it used to?" Once you've proven a capability, it becomes the floor — you don't regress from there. ([Anthropic on evals for AI agents](https://www.anthropic.com/research/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents#how-to-evaluate-ai-agents)) — a good post to understand AI agent evals. #goodread
