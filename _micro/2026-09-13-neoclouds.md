_**NeoClouds needed for the Frontier labs**_

[Chamath's deep dive on neoclouds](https://research.socialcapital.com/p/neoclouds) gives some good info on NeoClouds. These AI-native clouds rent GPU hours 50–70% below hyperscaler list prices and get earlier access to scarce Nvidia chips. The key difference from traditional clouds: AI training occupies thousands of GPUs in lockstep for 1–4 months at a time, rack power jumps from 10–15 kW to 40–250 kW, and neoclouds strip out the software layers that get in the way. Frontier labs are bypassing AWS/Azure/GCP for compute. The open question: can the economics survive when customers are also building their own capacity and custom chips?
