_**Crackdown on Subscriptions sharing**_

[Spotify confirmed](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/spotify-confirms-family-plan-crackdown-but-calls-it-a-routine-improvement/) it's rolling out QR-code household verification for Family Plan members &mdash; scan within 7 days or get locked out and downgraded to an individual plan. Spotify's co-CEO calls it "a routine improvement" to 13-year-old verification tooling. Everyone else is calling it what it is: a crackdown on sharing with friends and non-cohabiting family, aimed squarely at Premium revenue.

Same playbook Netflix ran on password sharing in 2023, framed as protecting creators while users experienced it as paying more for the access they already had. Call it what it actually is: a crackdown wearing an "improvement" badge.
