_**AI Is Already Finding Things in Auto Safety**_

Most of the AI talk I hear is about speed: faster reviews, fewer hours, cheaper reports. That's fine, but it's not the prize.

The real prize is AI that finds things, and I'm now seeing this work in practice, not just in theory. A cluster of owner complaints described in a dozen different ways. A warranty spike that quietly lines up with a new service bulletin. A trucking carrier whose inspection record is slowly getting worse. Signals like these sit across NHTSA, warranty and FMCSA data for months before anyone connects them. AI is helping build solutions to connect these signals, and the results have been great.

Catch them early and you change a recall decision, a case strategy, maybe a crash that never happens.

The catch: people have to see the reasoning. Otherwise AI just becomes a tool to demand lower rates.
