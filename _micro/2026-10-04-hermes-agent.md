_**Personal AI Agent -- Hermes**_

I have been running the [Hermes agent](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/)
on my Next laptop across multiple sessions. Its persistent memory is a cool feature which helps the agent store and retrieve from its memory between sessions. I can close a conversation and come back later without re-explaining the context. The agent remembers what we were doing.

Hermes is on a brand new installation of [Omarchy](https://omarchy.org/) Linux on a Lenovo laptop. Omarchy works great and I love it!

Hermes keeps getting smarter every day. It reaches for the skills we have built for different activities — the things I talk to Hermes about on a day-to-day basis — and turns them into real output, not just plans.

Hermes is like [OpenClaw](https://myclaw.ai/openclaw) (which took the tech world by storm in early 2026). Hostinger gives a nice [comparison](https://www.hostinger.com/tutorials/hermes-agent-vs-openclaw/) between Hermes and Openclaw.
