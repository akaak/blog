_**10x Engineer?**_

With Claude Code and other AI tools, the drudgery is out. No more keeping track of development environment quirks, package versions, API docs, broken devops, and incomplete/outdated project documentation. 
The stuff that the developers did not like doing and that ate hours but never felt like the actual work can be handled by the AI tools. There are many useful AI skills ([prepare tickets](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/engineering/to-tickets/SKILL.md), [debugging](https://github.com/addyosmani/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/debugging-and-error-recovery/SKILL.md), [documentation](https://github.com/addyosmani/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/documentation-and-adrs/SKILL.md)), that engineers can adopt or develop on their own to take the drudgery out of the software development. Engineers are good at streamlining things and they have really embraced all these skills. I use them as well and love it all.

What's left is the creative, productive part: deciding why & what to build, for whom, and why. Engineers, if wanted, can easily transition into the product management and marketing roles and attack the _why, what, whom and why_ as well now. New ways and new models. What do we call this? Nx Engineer?! 

New era, new slogan: **drudgery out, focus in.**
