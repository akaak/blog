_Wait... Are we are going to slow things down now?_

Caught up on [Pacing the Frontier](https://www.pacingthefrontier.com/) &mdash; the open statement signed by 1,100+ employees across Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and Meta asking the US government to back an international effort to deliberately pace frontier AI development. Notable that this isn't outside critics; it's people building the models themselves, including leadership at the labs, saying capability is outrunning the governance and safety tooling meant to keep up with it. [Reuters coverage here](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/tech-employees-call-us-backed-global-effort-manage-risks-advanced-ai-2026-07-28/).

Worth sitting with: the ask isn't to stop progress, it's to build the technical and governance muscle to pace it on purpose rather than by accident.
