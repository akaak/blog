_**New model in town. What's up Jev!**_

Been experimenting with [**Jev**](https://docs.typesafe.ai/introduction), Typesafe.ai's ["System 1"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thinking,_Fast_and_Slow) model &mdash; built for fast, intuitive responses rather than slow, deliberate reasoning. On its own it's quick and cheap, but pairing it with a heavier LLM turns out to be the real win for certain workflows: let Jev make the fast first pass &mdash; triage, routing, a quick draft or gut-check &mdash; and only hand off to a deeper reasoning model when the task actually needs System 2-style deliberation.

For high-volume, low-stakes steps this cuts latency and cost without giving up quality where it matters. Right tool, right layer.
